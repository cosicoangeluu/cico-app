About

What CICO is for

CICO helps the operator of a cash-in / cash-out counter record every transaction, its separate fee and its receipt, then look back at history and totals.

You

Made for the operator

The person behind the counter signs in and uses CICO. Customers don't need an account.

₱

Fees are never guessed

CICO works out the fee from your own fee table and always keeps it apart from the amount.

1×

Each transfer pays out once

A transfer reference already used on another cash-out is blocked, so cash can't go out twice.

CICO records transactions. It does not send or receive money. You still do every transfer in your own wallet app.

Workflows

Three ways money moves through your counter

Pick one to see each step. Cash never goes out until the money is found in your own wallet.

Cash-in

Customer gives you cash. You send it to their wallet.

Saved as completed straight away.

Example only: ₱1,000 with a ₱10 fee
Customer gives you
₱1,000 + ₱10 fee
You send to their wallet
₱1,000
  1. Customer

    Asks for a cash-in and tells you the amount and their wallet details.

  2. You

    Tap Cash-in on Home and type the amount.

  3. CICO

    Shows the fee from your fee rules, plus what the customer pays in total. Tell the customer the amount and the separate fee.

  4. Customer

    Agrees and pays you the amount plus the fee.

  5. You

    Send the full amount to the customer's wallet from your wallet app, and check that it went through.

  6. You

    Take a photo of the receipt or upload it (optional), then type the reference number, the customer's name, and the date and time of the transfer.

  7. You

    Tick both boxes: the customer paid, and your transfer went through.

  8. Done

    Tap Save cash-in. It's saved as completed and shows in History and Reports.

Cash-out

Customer sends to your wallet. You give them cash.

Check your own wallet before any cash leaves the drawer.

Example only: ₱1,000 with a ₱10 fee
Customer sends to your wallet
₱1,000
Customer pays you the fee
₱10
You give the customer
₱1,000 cash
  1. Customer

    Asks for a cash-out.

  2. You

    Tap Cash-out and type the amount. CICO shows the separate fee. Explain both; the customer agrees.

  3. You

    Under "Who is sending the money?" choose The customer here and type their name.

  4. Customer

    Sends the full amount to your wallet.

  5. Check first

    Open your own wallet history and find the transfer. The reference number and amount must match.

    Money not there yet? Choose Not yet to save it as pending. Don't hand over any cash.

  6. You

    Choose Yes, I can see it. Add the receipt photo if there is one, then type the reference number and the date and time of the transfer.

  7. You

    Collect the separate fee and hand over the full cash amount. Tick both boxes.

  8. Done

    Tap Complete cash-out. History and Reports update.

Someone else sends

The person collecting the cash is with you. Someone else sends the money.

Two people, two names on the record.

Who does what
Sender (remote)
sends to your wallet
Receiver (at your counter)
pays fee, gets cash
  1. Receiver

    Asks for a cash-out and tells you who will send the money.

  2. You

    Tap Cash-out, type the amount, and choose Someone else. Type the receiver's name, and the sender's name if you know it.

  3. You

    Choose Not yet and leave "Make a link the sender can use" ticked. Tap Save as pending. The fee is fixed now.

  4. You

    Open the pending cash-out and tap Copy link. The receiver shares it with the sender.

  5. Sender

    Transfers the money to your wallet, opens the link and uploads a photo of the receipt with their name (and the reference, if they like).

  6. You

    The receipt appears on the pending cash-out. Copy what the sender typed into the form, or type it yourself. Check both names.

  7. Check first

    Find the money in your own wallet history. A receipt alone isn't proof. CICO also blocks a reference already used on another cash-out.

  8. You

    Collect the fee from the receiver and hand over the full cash amount. Tick both boxes.

  9. Done

    Tap Complete cash-out. The sender's link stops working, and History and Reports update.

The separate fee

The fee is always on top, never taken out

One fee table covers both cash-in and cash-out. Each range starts just above the one before and charges a fixed fee, so there are no gaps.

Above your highest range, or with no ranges yet, CICO won't record it. Add a range in Fee rules first. Past records always keep the fee they were saved with.

Operator guide

Before any cash goes out, check your own wallet

Find the transfer in your own wallet history. The reference number and the amount must match. A receipt, a screenshot or an upload is not proof on its own.

CICO never asks for wallet PINs, passwords or one-time codes. Nobody needs them.

Good habits

The few rules that keep your drawer right

Short habits that keep every record and every peso where it should be.

Recording a transaction

  • Start from Home: Cash-in or Cash-out.
  • Type the amount first. The rest of the form appears once CICO has found the fee.
  • After saving, you can start the next one straight away.

Receipt review

  • Take a photo or Upload a picture. It stays beside the form.
  • Type the reference number exactly, including any zeroes at the start. CICO keeps every character.
  • Check the name, amount, date and time against the picture before you save.

Manual entry

  • A photo is never required.
  • No receipt? Open your wallet history and type the details from there.

Pending and completed

  • Pending: a cash-out waiting for money. No cash has been handed over. Find these on the Pending screen.
  • Completed: the money is checked, the fee is collected and the cash is handed over. It can't be paid out again.
  • Cash-ins are saved as completed straight away.

Reading reports

  • Pick Today, This week, This month or Choose dates.
  • Cash-in: cash you received and the same amount sent from your wallet. Cash-out: money received in your wallet and the cash you handed out.
  • Fees earned is your income. The amounts themselves are the customers' money passing through.

The sender's link

  • Each link belongs to one pending cash-out only.
  • The sender sees just the amount and the receiver's first name.
  • Once the cash-out is completed, the link stops working.
FAQ

Common questions

How CICO behaves in the cases that matter at the counter.

Who uses CICO?

The operator: the person running the cash-in / cash-out counter. Customers don't sign in. The only other person who ever opens a CICO page is a remote sender, and only to upload a receipt through a link you give them.

Does CICO transfer money, or record transactions done in the wallet?

It only records them. You send and receive money in your own wallet app as usual. CICO keeps the amount, the fee, the names, the reference and the receipt photo together.

How are fees calculated and paid?

You set them in Fee rules: a list of amount ranges, each with a fixed fee. The same table is used for cash-in and cash-out. When you type an amount, CICO finds the matching range and shows the fee.

The fee is always paid separately, on top of the amount. It is never taken out of what the customer sends or receives. If an amount is above your highest range, or there are no ranges yet, CICO won't record it until you add one.

Do changed fees affect past records?

No. Every record keeps the fee it was saved with, and History shows the fee range that was used. A pending cash-out also keeps the fee agreed when it was created. New fees apply only to new transactions.

What if receipt scanning reads the details incorrectly?

Scanning isn't available yet, so CICO doesn't read the receipt for you. You type the details while looking at the photo, and every field can be corrected before you save. When scanning arrives, its results will be just as editable.

What if there is no receipt?

That's fine. A photo is never required. Find the transfer in your wallet history and type the reference number, name, and date and time from there.

Can someone else send money for the person collecting cash?

Yes. In Cash-out, choose Someone else under "Who is sending the money?". CICO keeps both names: the sender and the person collecting the cash. See the Someone else sends workflow.

How do receipt-upload links and QR codes work?

When you save a "someone else" cash-out as pending, CICO can make a link for that one cash-out. Copy it from the pending cash-out and have the receiver pass it to the sender.

  • The sender sees only the amount and the receiver's first name.
  • They upload a photo of the receipt, their name and, if they like, the reference.
  • They can't see anything else in CICO.
  • Once the cash-out is completed, the link stops working.

QR codes aren't available yet. In demo mode, the link only works in the same browser on the same computer.

Does an uploaded receipt prove payment arrived?

No. A receipt can be wrong, old or fake. An upload never completes a cash-out on its own. Always find the money in your own wallet history first.

When should the operator release cash?

Only after you have found the transfer in your own wallet history and the reference number and amount match. Then collect the separate fee, hand over the full cash amount, tick both boxes and tap Complete cash-out.

What happens if a reference has already been used?

CICO won't let you complete the cash-out. It tells you which cash-out already has that reference, so you can check your wallet history. References are compared ignoring spaces and capital letters, so "ab 123" and "AB123" count as the same. Each transfer can only be paid out once.

What is the difference between pending and completed?

Pending (yellow) is a cash-out still waiting for the money to arrive. No cash should have been handed over. Completed means the money was checked, the fee was collected and the cash was handed over, or for cash-in, your transfer went through. A completed cash-out can't be paid out again.

Which transactions count in reports?

Only completed ones, counted on the day they were completed (Philippine time). Pending cash-outs are shown as a number only and never added to the totals or to fees earned. Amounts and fees are always shown separately, and only the fees are your income.
